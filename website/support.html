Compatibility

  • iPhone and iPad require iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.
  • The Mac version runs through Mac Catalyst.
  • Handwriting uses PencilKit; Apple Pencil features require compatible iPad hardware.
  • Apple Intelligence note generation requires supported hardware, a compatible OS, and Apple Intelligence enabled and ready.
  • Google Drive sync requires a configured Google account and network connection.

Common questions

Does Paperly require an account?

No Paperly account is required. You can create and edit documents locally. Google Sign-In is only used if you choose optional Google Drive synchronization.

Does Paperly work offline?

Local writing, drawing, document organization, and previously prepared on-device features work offline. Initial model downloads and Google Drive sync require a network connection.

Why can’t I create an audio note?

Confirm Audio notes is enabled in Paperly Settings and microphone access is allowed. Structured note generation requires an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence. Keep Paperly open after a long recording until local processing finishes.

Where are my notes stored?

Paperly saves locally first inside the app’s device storage. If you connect Google Drive, portable Paperly workspace files are also exchanged through your Google account.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area on the iPhone Home Screen, choose Edit, then Add Widget and search for Paperly. Lock Screen widget placement is available through Lock Screen customization.

How do I report a sync problem?

Include the affected device models, OS versions, whether both devices use the same Google account, and the exact status Paperly shows. Do not send private document contents unless support specifically needs a sample.
