Transcribed and structured on device

Private by architecture,
not by promise.

Paperly stores your work locally first. Handwriting recognition, audio transcription, and note generation run on supported Apple devices—not on a Paperly server.

Read the privacy policy →
No Paperly account
Open the app and start writing. There is no Paperly backend account to create.
No analytics
Paperly does not include advertising trackers or product analytics.
Optional Drive sync
Connect Google Drive only if you choose. Local editing does not depend on it.
Recoverable files
Editable notes, ink, audio, and transcripts stay portable in Paperly’s document format.

Made for the device
already in your hand.

Paperly adapts instead of stretching one interface everywhere. Capture on iPhone, write with Apple Pencil on iPad, and work through longer documents on Mac.

iPhone

Capture anywhere.

Text, search, folders, widgets, and focused audio notes on supported iPhones.

iPad

Write without friction.

Handwriting, PDF markup, images, page templates, and native PencilKit tools.

Mac

Shape the longer thought.

Organize the same workspace, edit text, and turn local recordings into structured notes.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. Some audio-note intelligence features require compatible Apple Intelligence hardware and software.
