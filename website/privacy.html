Privacy policy

Paperly is designed to keep your notes on your devices. This policy explains what the app accesses, what stays local, and what changes if you choose to connect Google Drive.

Summary

  • Paperly does not operate an account server, analytics service, or advertising system.
  • Your documents are stored locally first on your device.
  • Handwriting recognition, transcription, and supported Apple Intelligence features run on-device.
  • Google Drive sync is optional and only begins after you connect a Google account.

Information Paperly handles

Paperly handles the content you create or import, including text, handwriting, PDFs, images, audio recordings, transcripts, document titles, folders, and editing metadata. This content is needed to provide the app’s notebook, search, export, recovery, and synchronization features.

Local storage and on-device processing

Documents are written to app storage on your device before any optional sync. Apple Vision handwriting recognition, Whisper transcription, and Apple Foundation Models note generation run locally on supported hardware. Paperly does not send this content to a Paperly-operated AI or analytics server.

Device permissions

Paperly may request access only when you use a related feature:

  • Microphone: to record audio notes.
  • Photos or files: when you choose an image, PDF, or Paperly document to import.
  • Network: to download an on-device transcription model and to use Google services you connect.

You can change permissions in the Settings app. Features requiring a denied permission will remain unavailable until access is restored.

Optional Google Drive connection

If you connect Google Drive, Paperly uses Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API to identify the selected account and exchange Paperly workspace files. Data sent through that feature is processed by Google under your Google account settings and Google’s privacy terms. Disconnecting Drive stops future sync but does not automatically delete files already stored locally or in Drive.

Data sharing

Paperly does not sell your personal information. The app does not include third-party advertising or product analytics. Information is shared with Google only when you choose the Google Drive connection, or with a destination you select through Apple’s system share and export controls.

Retention and deletion

Local content remains until you delete it through Paperly, remove the app and its data, or erase the device. Items moved to Trash can be restored until removed according to the app’s available deletion controls. Google Drive copies remain subject to actions and retention in your Google account.

Children’s privacy

Paperly is a general-purpose productivity app and is not directed to children under 13. Because Paperly does not operate an account or analytics backend, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Paperly service.

Security

Paperly relies on Apple platform protections, app sandboxing, and your device security settings for local data. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure; keep your devices and connected accounts protected and current.

Policy changes

Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. The current draft is dated October 23, 2026.
