OWN-WORLD: White and cool pale blue, decisive electric-blue actions, near-black Geist typography, thin rules, square controls, rounded screenshot frames, and unaltered dark app screenshots. The user also requested rounded product screenshots: 16px desktop, 10px mobile, and 8px for the mobile phone preview.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A floating translucent wordmark/navigation bar above a large left title and rectangular blue Get Paperly action. A large iPad handwriting screenshot anchors the right side on a pale-blue field; a restrained phone screenshot overlaps its lower edge. Large type, generous negative space, and a compact license statement carry the free/open-source position without badges. The gallery uses an underlined tab rail and a real screenshot, with restrained cross-fades and keyboard controls.

FORM: Open product desk, authored from the user’s pinned product screenshots and explicit visual requests. The earlier seed roll has no retained output and is not treated as verified direction provenance; no external quality-bar card is claimed. The user's modern, no-pill, original-screenshot constraints govern the composition. Foreign retro/ornamental catalog directions were declined for product clarity; retain their discipline of consistent navigation, structural honesty, and scale without importing decorative motifs. The pinned real-screenshot direction supersedes generated comps.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance


MOTION: Motion for React (Framer Motion) reveals the real hero screenshots with opacity and a 3px settle; gallery changes crossfade and the active underline travels between tabs.
